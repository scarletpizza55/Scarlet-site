About us

One address. Three kitchens. No compromises.

How a deli counter on the Rutgers campus became a one-stop destination for the whole neighborhood.

Our story

Built for the way campus actually eats

Anyone who has tried to feed a group of college students knows the problem. One person wants pizza. One wants tacos. One is vegetarian. One only eats halal. Someone else just wants a breakfast sandwich at four in the afternoon.

Scarlet Deli & Pizza was built to solve exactly that. We combine classic deli favorites — the overstuffed heroes, the burgers, the by-the-slice pizza — with two full restaurant concepts under the same roof: Pizza Twist for Indian fusion and traditional pizza, and Taco Twist for fusion tacos, burritos and Indian-inspired street food.

Your whole group orders from one place. Nobody has to compromise, and nobody has to split up across three storefronts.

The three concepts

What’s under the roof

Scarlet Deli & Pizza

Breakfast from early morning, deli heroes and burgers through the day, pizza by the slice and whole pies, plus salads, wraps, desserts and cold drinks. The everyday anchor of the building.

Pizza Twist

Indian fusion pizza done properly — tandoori, tikka and masala flavors on hand-tossed crusts, next to the traditional pies. A dedicated halal menu, plus vegetarian options throughout.

Taco Twist

Fusion tacos, burritos and bowls with Indian-inspired street food running through the menu. Built to order, heavy on spice, fast out the window.

Our location

Right where you already are

We’re at 51 Paul Robeson Blvd in New Brunswick, a short walk from College Ave and on the bus route from the other campuses. Close enough for a quick lunch between classes, open late enough for after the library closes.

That location shapes how we run. Service is quick, portions are generous, prices are student-friendly, and the doors stay open long after most kitchens in town have shut down.
