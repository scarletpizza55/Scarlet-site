A one-stop destination

Three kitchens. One counter. Whatever you’re in the mood for.

Scarlet Deli & Pizza brings together three distinct restaurant concepts in a single spot on the Rutgers campus. Grab a breakfast sandwich before class, a deli hero at lunch, an Indian fusion pizza at dinner, and fusion tacos late at night — without your group having to agree on one cuisine.

It’s built for how people on a college campus actually eat: fast, varied and at every hour of the day. Fresh ingredients, generous portions, and vegetarian choices across every concept.
